What is Kavach?

Real examples

Exactly what you'll receive

SCAM
"CBI officer here. Your Aadhaar is linked to money laundering. You are under digital arrest. Stay on this video call and tell no one."

यह "डिजिटल अरेस्ट" स्कैम है। भारत में ऐसा कोई कानून नहीं है — CBI इस तरह वीडियो कॉल पर गिरफ्तार नहीं करती।

→ कॉल काट दें। घर के किसी सदस्य को बताएं। 1930 पर कॉल करें।

SUSPICIOUS
"Your SBI KYC is incomplete. Update in 24 hours or your account will be frozen: sbi-kyc-update.xyz"

The link "sbi-kyc-update.xyz" is not an official SBI domain. Banks never send KYC links by SMS or WhatsApp.

→ Go directly to sbi.co.in or call the number on your bank card to check.

SAFE
"Dear Customer, your Amazon order #408-3746291 has shipped. Track at amazon.in. Delivery in 3 days."

This looks like a genuine Amazon shipping update. The format and link match Amazon's real messages.

→ Safe to track. To be sure, open the order in the Amazon app rather than the link.
