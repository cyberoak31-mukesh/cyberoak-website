Real Verdict Examples

Exactly what you'll receive

🚨 SCAM
"CBI officer speaking. Your Aadhaar is linked to money laundering. You are under Digital Arrest. Don't tell anyone or you will be arrested in 2 hours."

यह "Digital Arrest" स्कैम है — जो भारत में सबसे खतरनाक स्कैम है। CBI या कोई भी सरकारी संस्था WhatsApp पर इस तरह contact नहीं करती।

🚨 अभी करें: Call disconnect करें। अपने घर के किसी सदस्य को बताएं। पुलिस को 1930 पर call करें।
⚠️ SUSPICIOUS
"Your SBI account KYC is incomplete. Click to update in 24 hours or your account will be frozen: sbi-kyc-update.xyz"

The link domain "sbi-kyc-update.xyz" is not SBI's official website. Banks never send KYC update links via SMS or WhatsApp.

🔍 Do this now: Go directly to sbi.co.in or call SBI's official helpline 1800-11-2211 to check your KYC status.
✅ SAFE
"Dear Customer, your Amazon order #408-3746291 has been shipped. Track at amazon.in/tracking. Expected delivery: 3 days."

This appears to be a legitimate Amazon shipping notification. The format, sender, and link match Amazon's genuine communication style.

✅ Safe to track: The amazon.in link is safe to click. Verify your order number in the Amazon app to be sure.

Every Kavach verdict has exactly 4 parts:

Line 1: Verdict
SCAM / SUSPICIOUS / SAFE — always clear, always first
Lines 2–3: Reason
Max 2 sentences. Simple words. A 65-year-old can understand.
Line 4: Action
One specific thing to do RIGHT NOW. Not a list — one thing.
Line 5: Disclaimer
One-line guidance notice + link to full terms. Never clutters the verdict.
