Know Your Enemy

India's Most Dangerous Scam Types

Understanding how each scam works is your best defence.

👮

Digital Arrest Scam — Most Dangerous in 2024

A caller claims to be a CBI/ED/Police/Narcotics officer. They say your Aadhaar, phone, or bank account is linked to a crime. They demand you stay on a video call (so you can't call family) and pay lakhs to "settle the case." No government agency in India conducts "digital arrests" or demands payment to avoid arrest over a video call.

🚨
If you receive such a call: Hang up immediately. Call 1930. Tell a family member right away. Do NOT pay anything.
🆔

KYC / Bank Account Freeze Scam

A message or call claims your bank KYC is incomplete and your account will be frozen within 24 hours. They provide a fake link or phone number. Clicking gives them access to your banking credentials. Real banks send KYC notices by registered post or via their official app — never via unsolicited SMS links.

💸

UPI Collect Request Scam

Scammer sends a UPI collect request (₹1, ₹10, or sometimes large amounts) claiming it's a refund, prize, or cashback. When you "approve" the request, you're actually sending money — not receiving it. Approving a UPI collect request always means you are paying, not receiving.

📞

Fake Customer Care Number

Scammers pay to appear in Google search results for "SBI customer care", "Amazon refund number" etc. When you call, they ask for OTP or remote access to your phone. Always find bank/company numbers on the back of your card or official website — never via Google search.

📈

Investment / Trading App Fraud

WhatsApp groups or Telegram channels with fake "stock tips" or "crypto trading platforms" that show artificial profits. When you invest more, the "profits" are frozen and you're asked to pay "tax" or "withdrawal fees" to access your money. Legitimate trading apps are regulated by SEBI and registered brokers — not WhatsApp groups.

🎙️

Deepfake Voice / AI Clone Scam

AI technology can now clone a family member's voice from just 3 seconds of audio. You receive a call from "your son/daughter" saying they're in trouble and need money urgently. Always call back on the family member's original number — don't send money based on a single call.

Your Safety Checklist

10 Rules to Never Break

Print this page and put it on your parents' fridge.

1
Never share OTP with anyone — not with "bank officials", not with "family", not with anyone. Ever.
2
Approving a UPI request means you are PAYING — never "receive" money by approving a request.
3
No government arrests you over video call — CBI, ED, Police all follow legal physical procedures.
4
KBC / lottery winnings are NOT real — you cannot win a prize you never entered.
5
Urgency is the scammer's weapon — if someone says "do it in the next 2 hours or else", pause and call a trusted person first.
6
Bank numbers on cards, not Google — search results can show scammer numbers.
7
Never install apps from unknown links — legitimate apps are only on Play Store / App Store.
8
Unusual family emergency calls — verify first — call back on the known number before sending any money.
9
Save 1930 — National Cybercrime Helpline in your phone contacts right now.
10
When in doubt — ask Kavach — forward the message to +91 8624892131 before you act.

Government & Official Helplines

📞

Cybercrime Helpline: 1930

24/7 helpline. Call within 30 minutes of being scammed for the best chance of recovering money. Operated by Ministry of Home Affairs.

🌐

cybercrime.gov.in

National Cybercrime Reporting Portal. File detailed complaints, track status, and report financial fraud. Operated by Government of India.

📱

Sanchar Saathi Portal

Block your stolen/lost SIM, report fraudulent calls, and check how many SIMs are registered on your Aadhaar: sancharsaathi.gov.in

🏦

RBI Helpline: 14440

Reserve Bank of India's Integrated Ombudsman Scheme helpline for banking and payment-related fraud complaints.
