2. The Service

CyberOak provides the Kavach bot — an AI-powered tool that analyses messages, URLs, and phone numbers to help users determine if they may be scams. The Service is available free of charge for individual use.

What Kavach does:

  • Analyses forwarded messages using AI (Google Gemini)
  • Checks URLs against VirusTotal and Google Safe Browsing databases
  • Provides a verdict: SCAM, SUSPICIOUS, or SAFE
  • Provides emergency guidance if a user reports being actively scammed

Fair Use Limits:

  • Maximum 20 message checks per user per day
  • The Service is for personal, non-commercial use only
  • Automated or bulk use is prohibited

4. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and the Information Technology Rules 2021 of India.

What information do we collect?

When you use the Kavach bot, we may collect:

  • Phone number hash / Telegram user ID: Used to enforce rate limits (20/day) and to associate your data with your rights request. We store a one-way hash, not your actual number.
  • Timestamp: When you sent a message, to manage daily limits.
  • Verdict metadata: What verdict was given (SCAM/SUSPICIOUS/SAFE) — not the message content — for service improvement analytics.
  • Website analytics: Standard anonymised page visit data via Google Analytics (if enabled) — no personal identifiers.

5. What We Do NOT Store

  • The content of messages you forward to Kavach is NOT stored permanently. It is processed in memory to generate the verdict and then discarded.
  • We do NOT store your name, Aadhaar number, PAN, bank details, or any sensitive personal data.
  • We do NOT record or store voice notes.
  • We do NOT link your verdicts to your real identity.

How long do we keep data?

  • Rate-limit records (phone number hash + timestamp): 24 hours, then automatically deleted.
  • Aggregated, anonymised analytics: Up to 12 months.
  • Support emails sent to help@cyberoak.in: Retained for up to 3 years for grievance resolution.

Do we sell your data?

No. We never sell, rent, or trade any user data to any third party for commercial purposes.

6. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

As a user, you have the following rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of what personal data we hold about you.

7. Third-Party Services

CyberOak uses the following third-party services to provide the Service. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Gemini AI: Message content is sent to Google's Gemini API for analysis. Google's data processing agreement applies. We use data minimisation — only the message is sent, no user identity.
  • VirusTotal / Google Safe Browsing: URLs in messages are checked against these databases. Only the URL (not your identity) is shared.
  • Wati.io: WhatsApp Business API provider. Messages on WhatsApp pass through Wati.io's infrastructure. Subject to their privacy policy.
  • Google Cloud / Firestore: Service infrastructure. Data is stored in Google Cloud's asia-south1 (Mumbai) region.
  • Telegram: Telegram bot messages pass through Telegram's servers. Subject to Telegram's privacy policy.
