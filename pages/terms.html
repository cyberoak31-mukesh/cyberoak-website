2. The Service

CyberOak provides the Kavach bot — a multi-layer scam awareness system that analyses messages, URLs, phone numbers, images, and voice notes using a combination of threat databases, pattern detection, and automated analysis methods.

What Kavach does:

  • Analyses forwarded messages using multi-layer detection
  • Checks URLs against threat intelligence databases
  • Provides a verdict: SCAM, SUSPICIOUS, or SAFE
  • Provides emergency guidance if you report being actively scammed
  • Analyses images and voice notes forwarded by users

5. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and the Information Technology Rules 2021 of India.

What we collect:

  • User identifier (phone number hash or Telegram ID) — for rate limiting and rights requests only
  • Timestamp of each interaction
  • Verdict given (SCAM / SUSPICIOUS / SAFE)
  • A short preview of the message (up to 200 characters) — for service quality and safety improvement only
  • Aggregated threat indicators (phone numbers, URLs, domains) extracted from messages — stored without any link to your identity

6. What We Do NOT Store

  • Full message content beyond the 200-character preview
  • Your name, Aadhaar number, PAN, bank details, or any sensitive personal data
  • Voice note content or audio recordings
  • Any data that links your real identity to your verdicts
  • Location data of any kind

How long we keep data:

  • Rate-limit records: 24 hours, then automatically deleted
  • Message previews and verdict metadata: up to 12 months, then deleted
  • Threat indicators (aggregated, anonymised): up to 24 months
  • Support emails: up to 3 years for grievance resolution

Do we sell your data?

No. We never sell, rent, or trade any user data to any third party for commercial purposes. The only information we share is aggregated, anonymised threat data (such as scam phone numbers and malicious URLs) used to improve scam detection — never linked to your identity, and never for advertising.

To provide the Service, CyberOak uses trusted third-party infrastructure providers. Your data is handled as follows:

Forwarded messages are processed by our automated detection system.
