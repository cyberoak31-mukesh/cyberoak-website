The Story Behind CyberOak

Why I Built This

" I have spent close to two decades in cybersecurity. In that time, I have protected banks, enterprises, and critical systems from some of the most sophisticated attacks you can imagine. I know exactly how criminals operate online — the tactics, the psychology, the technical tricks.

None of that prepared me for watching someone I know cry after losing everything to a WhatsApp scam.

It wasn't a lack of intelligence that got them. It was a lack of access — to the right information, at the right moment, in a language they understood. The scammer called at 9pm with urgency and fear. There was nobody to call back and say: "This is fake. Put the phone down."

That's the gap CyberOak fills. Not a complicated app. Not a paid subscription. Just a WhatsApp number you can forward anything to — and get a straight answer within 60 seconds, in Hindi or English, any time of day or night.

I built the same quality of threat intelligence I'd deploy for a large enterprise — and put it in the hands of the person who needs it most. Your parents. Your neighbours. Anyone who deserves protection but can't afford a cybersecurity consultant.

Simply to help. That's the whole reason.

Mukesh Bhardwaj
Founder & CEO, CyberOak · Pune, Maharashtra
