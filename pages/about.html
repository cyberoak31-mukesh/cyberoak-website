The Story Behind CyberOak

Why I Built This

" I have spent 15 years defending organisations worth hundreds of crores from cyber attacks. I know exactly how these threats work, how attackers think, and how to stop them.

But for all that expertise — when ordinary people around me were losing their life savings to a scammer on WhatsApp, I felt helpless. Not because I didn't know the answer. But because they had no way to reach me at 11pm when the scammer called.

I have seen people cry. Not young tech-savvy people — but parents, grandparents, retired teachers, farmers, shop owners who spent 30 years building their savings — losing it all in one phone call because they didn't know that "Digital Arrest" isn't a real thing.

The knowledge to protect yourself exists. It just isn't accessible. Not in simple Hindi. Not on WhatsApp where ordinary Indians actually spend their time. Not available at 2am when the scammer calls.

CyberOak is my attempt to fix that. To make enterprise-grade scam detection available to the person who can't afford a cybersecurity consultant — and shouldn't need one.

Simply to help. That's why.

Mukesh Bhardwaj
Founder, CyberOak · Pune, Maharashtra
