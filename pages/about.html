Our story

Built by a security expert.
Driven by empathy.

CyberOak wasn't built as a business idea. It was built because people were losing their life savings — and someone with the skills to help decided to.

Mukesh Bhardwaj

Founder & CEO · CyberOak

For nearly two decades, Mukesh has been the person organisations call when they're under attack. He has led threat detection and response, incident investigations, and enterprise-wide security programmes — defending critical systems against sophisticated, well-funded adversaries.

What he couldn't defend against was the scammer calling his neighbour at 9pm, posing as the CBI. Or the WhatsApp message that nearly convinced a retired teacher to part with her savings for a "prize." That gap — between enterprise-grade security knowledge and the ordinary person holding a phone — is exactly what CyberOak exists to close.

He built Kavach the way he'd protect a large enterprise — with real threat intelligence and live link analysis — except this time it's free, it lives on WhatsApp, and it's built for your parents too.

The reason

Why I built this

I've spent close to two decades in cybersecurity, protecting enterprises and critical systems from sophisticated attacks. I know how criminals operate online — the tactics, the psychology, the technical tricks.

None of that prepared me for watching someone I know break down after losing everything to a scam on WhatsApp.

It wasn't a lack of intelligence that got them. It was a lack of access — to the right information, at the right moment, in a language they understood. The scammer called with urgency and fear, and there was no one to call back and say, simply: "This is fake. Put the phone down."

That's the gap CyberOak fills. Not a complicated app. Not a subscription. Just a number you can forward anything to, and get a straight answer in under a minute — in Hindi or English, any time of day or night.

I put the same quality of threat intelligence I'd deploy for a large enterprise into the hands of the person who needs it most: your parents, your neighbours, anyone who deserves protection but can't afford a consultant.

Simply to help. That's the whole reason.

Mukesh Bhardwaj
Founder & CEO, CyberOak · Pune, Maharashtra
What we stand for

Mission & vision

Our mission

To make professional-grade scam detection available to every ordinary Indian — regardless of technical knowledge, age or language — through the apps they already use, at no cost.

Our vision

A Bharat where no family loses their savings to a digital scam, because everyone has a trusted, always-available place to check before they act.

Where we're headed

Our commitment to you

Always India-first. Every feature is designed around the realities of fraud in India, not copied from elsewhere.
Free for individuals, always. Core scam-checking stays free for every person. Protection shouldn't be a privilege.
Built around the most vulnerable. Every choice is tested against one question: can a 70-year-old use this without help?
Privacy by design. We never sell your data, never monetise it, and never compromise the trust you place in us.
